# R2.GearCalc — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

R2.GearCalc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The app has no analytics, no advertising, no
  tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- **No account.** The app works fully offline and requires no registration.
- **Local storage only.** Your settings (gearbox, gear sets, tire size,
  owned inventory) are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  Deleting the app deletes this data.
- **No network access.** The app does not send or receive any data over the
  internet.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this
page before the change takes effect.
